## Quick summary

- **Sequence:** `6, 14, 62, 1260`
- **Meaning:** dimension of the level-`n` truncation of the Lie algebra
  generated under the Poisson bracket by the C(4,2) = 6 pairwise
  interaction Hamiltonians of a 4-body system with a singular pairwise
  potential.
- **Computed:** all four terms exact; `a(3) = 1260` was the headline
  computation, completed 2026-04-11 on a 31-worker r6i.8xlarge AWS
  instance (3140s / 52 min).
- **Universality (through level 2, `[6, 14, 62]`):** confirmed for
  V(r) in {1/r^2, 1/r^3, log(r)}, in spatial dimensions d in
  {1, 2, 3}, and at three unequal-mass configurations. `a(3) = 1260`
  is confirmed only for V = 1/r.
- **OEIS novelty:** `seq:6,14,62,1260` returns no results
  (checked 2026-04-21).
